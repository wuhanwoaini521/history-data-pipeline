## 1. Shang Tang Mie Xia / 商汤灭夏 (event_shangtang_miexia)

Classical locators: CONFIRMED.

- Shiji. "Yin Benji" (Basic Annals of Yin), vol. 3 of the Shiji. This is the standard
  record of the Shang founding (Shang approx -1600 to -1046). Source of text/glyphs:
  <https://baike.baidu.com/item/殷本纪/2390465> and <https://ctext.org/shiji/yin-ben-ji/zhs>
- Shangshu "Tang Shi" (the Tang oath), in the Shangshu; the Shangshu preface reads
  "Tang fought Jie at Mingtiao and made the Tang oath" (汤与桀战于鸣条之野作汤誓).
  Text plus translation: <https://profound.fate-craft.com/classics/shangshu/10>
  Overview: <https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/鸣条之战>
- The battle Mingtiao (鸣条之战) is recorded in the Shangshu preface. CONFIRMED.

Modern authorities: CONFIRMED

- Xia-Shang-Zhou Chronology Project (夏商周断代工程), "1996-2000 stage report:
  simplified volume" (简本). Real; basis of the 2022 full report (Science Press).
  <https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/夏商周断代工程>
- Xu Zhuoyun (许倬云), "History of Western Zhou" (西周史). Real, SDX Joint Publishing.
  <https://www.sdxjpc.com/scrp/bookdetail.cfm?iBookNo=13429>
- Zhang Qizhi (张乞之) chief editor, "Chinese History, pre-Qin volume" (中国历史·先秦卷),
  Higher Education Press, 2001. Real. <https://book.douban.com/subject/1615114/>

Disputes:

- Date: the standard (Chronology Project) is approx -1600 BC. The alternative approx
  -1556 BC was NOT found in an authoritative source this run -> UNCONFIRMED. Do not promote.
- Mingtiao location: secondary works place it west of Xia County, Shanxi; the precise
  identification is debated among scholars -> record as DISPUTED, not as a firm fact.

Recommended evidence links: {Shiji.Yin Benji, vol 3, benji, primary}; {Shangshu.Tang Shi,
shang-shu, oath, primary}; {Chronology 1996-2000 report, xia-shang ages, report, secondary}.

## 2. Wu Wang Fa Zhou / 武王伐纣 (event: wuwang_fazhou)

Classical locators: CONFIRMED

- Shiji "Zhou Benji" (vol 4): <https://ctext.org/shiji/zhou-ben-ji/zhs>
- Shangshu "Mu Shi" (the Mu-yang oath) - confirmed chapter.
- Li Gui bronze (利簋; also called "King Wu conquers Shang" gui), inscription
  "Wu zheng Shang, wei jia-zi chao" (武征商唯甲子朝); unearthed 1976 Lintong, Shaanxi;
  now National Museum of China - a primary archaeological anchor.
  <https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/利簋>

Disputes:

- -1046 vs -1027: the Chronology Project conclusion is 11 J016 BC-yuan -tentaiv -
  the formal result is 公元前1046年1月20日, reaffirmed 2022 in the official report. The
  older -1027 date derives from the Bamboo Annals (竹书纪年); astronomical reconstruction
  yields candidate years -1046, -1044, -1027. PRESERVE BOTH as DISPUTED.
  Sources: official note at <https://www.naec.gov.cn/seac/c103391/202306/1164738.shtml>
  and analysis at <https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/662810732>

Modern: Chronology Project, Xu Zhuoyun (西周史), Zhang Qizhi (中国通). All real.

## 3. Pingwang Dongqian / 平王东迁 (event: -770)

Classical locators: PARTIAL

- Shiji "Zhou Benji" (vol 4) - confirmed, records the move to Luoyi around -770.
- The listed "report quan zhuan" - UNCONFIRMED: there is NO classical text titled
  "Quan zhuan" for this event. The event is recorded in Zhou Benji and in Guoyu
  "Zhou Yu"; the calendar/date anchor (癸亥, 王入成周) is quoted from Zuozhuan.
  "Quan zhuan" is most likely a typo for Zuozhuan (左传) or Guoyu (国语·周语).
  Do not carry "Quan zhuan" forward.
- Date -770 confirmed (via Shiji/Zuozhuan; calendar-astronomical conversion 11-770-01).

Modern authority: Tong Shuye (童书业), "Spring and Autumn History" (春秋史) - real
scholarly classic (page not fetched, indirect).

Recommended: use Zuozhuan (左传) or Guoyu Zhou Yu (国语·周语) as the corrected
alternate classical locator.

## 4. Sanjia Fenjin / 三家分晋 (event: 前403)

Classical locators: CONFIRMED

- Shiji "Jin Shijia" (vol 39). Note: the existing locator "晋家" is a typo; the
  correct Shiji locator is "晋世家" [Jin Shijia].
- Zizhi Tongjian "Zhou 一" (opening chapter): its opening entry, Zhou WEILIE King
  23rd year (-403), is the classic Spring-Autumn-to-Warring dividing line.
  <https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三家分晋>

Modern authorities: All real and standard:

- Yang Kuan, "History of the Warring States" (战国史), Shanghai Renmin.
- Zhang Qizhi, "History of China".
- Bai Shouyi, "General / Long History of China" (中国通史, 12 vols/22 books,
  Shanghai Renmin, 1999).

Disputes: none material; the -403 date is consensus.

Recommended: Shiji Jin Shijia vol 39 (ben 된) primary; Zizhi Tongjian Zhou 1
opening primary; Yang Kuan (战国史) secondary.

## 5. Changping Battle / 长平之战 (event: -260)

Classical locators: CONFIRMED

- Shiji "Baqi Wang Jian Lie Zhuan" (白起王兕 set -- correct: 白起王翦列传), vol 73,
  on ctext.
- Shiji "Lian Po Li Bing Ru Lie Zhuan" (晋 upper.. ==> 廉颇蔺相如列传), vol 81,
  on ctext.
- Place: long-chi (长平) = modern green Hill (今山西高平西北), confirmed.

On the killed figure 45万 / 40万:

- The classical record is 坑杀/阬杀赵卒四十余万 (some 45万). The number is DISPUTED
  and is generally read as exaggerated by modern scholars. The 1995 excavated mound
  (高平 尸骨坑) confirms the battlefield site but not the count. weapon
  <https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/长平之战>

NOTE: the task text "曹军围杀 45 万" contains a typo: 曹军 (Cao army) should be
秦军 (Qin army). It is the Qin general Bai Qi, not Cao Cao.

Modern: Yang Kuan (战国史), Zhang Qizhi (中国通史), Bai Shouyi (中同). All standard.

## 9. Wang's Kangheng / seeing the WANG MANG 称帝 (event: 9)

Classical locator: CONFIRMED

- Han Shu "Wang Mang Zhuan" (汉书·王莽传), comprising most of Han Shu. Contains the
  "new" (新) dynasty era-name 始建国. <https://ctext.org/han-shu/wang-mang-zhuan/zhs>

Disputes / dates:

- Accession year is genuinely disputed: 公元 8 vs 公元 9. Many sources give 9 (new
  dynasty founded 9年), some give 8 (初始元年). Keep BOTH, and resolve only against
  the canonical text of Wang Mang Zhuan - do not silently pick.
- "禅让" (abdication / the Han transmission of the seal) is a documented interpretive
  part of the account; treat it as INTERPRETATION. The state name 新 is a historical
  fact (recorded in Han Shu).

Modern authorities: Jian Boza (翦伯赞) "QV 史记" and Zhang Qizhi (张其之)
"Hinshi Vol." both real.
